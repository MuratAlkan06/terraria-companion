## TO-VERIFY-IN-P2 resolutions

- **#2 recipe-group encoding** — Cargo Recipes stores a group ingredient as its plain English name ("¦Any Iron Bar¦" in `ingredients`, "Any Iron Bar¦2" in `args`, "¦Any Iron Bar¦2" in `ings`); members are not in Cargo. Members come from Module:Recipes `group("Any …", {names})` (what the {{recipes}} template uses), resolved to IDs via Module:Iteminfo/data and cross-checked against Module:RecipeGroups (the game's SetupRecipeGroups, v1.4.5.4 transcription). Station alternatives (B3) come from Module:Recipes/l10n `en.station_cate` ("Iron Anvil" → "Iron or Lead Anvil").
- **#3 drop NPC ids / multi-part bosses** — Cargo Drops `id` carries NPC ids for most NPC rows (single, comma lists, ranges, per-variant notes, negative variant netIDs); container rows (crates, chests, treasure bags) carry none or the boss id.
